FAQ

Questions students ask

Can I book for early morning classes?

Yes. Choose the scheduled ride option and select a time ahead of your class.

Are fares fixed?

Yes. Fares are based on zone-based routes and are shown before confirmation.

How do I know my driver is reliable?

Drivers are verified, rated by riders, and clearly identified before pickup.

Does Rider work for groups?

Yes. You can book one to four passengers on the same trip.
